# AgentSupport Customer Return, Refund & Warranty Policy

## Overview

We are committed to ensuring every customer has a positive experience. If you are not fully satisfied with your purchase, our support team is here to help. This policy outlines the terms and procedures for returns, refunds, exchanges, and warranty claims.

## Return Policy

Customers may return most items within **30 days of the delivery date** for a full refund or exchange. To be eligible for a return, the item must be in its original condition, unused, and in the original packaging with all accessories and documentation included. Items that show signs of use, physical damage, or missing components may be subject to a restocking fee of up to 20% of the purchase price.

To initiate a return, contact our support team with your order ID and reason for return. You will receive a prepaid return shipping label by email within 1 business day. Once the returned item is received and inspected at our warehouse, we will process your refund or exchange within 2 business days.

## Refund Timeline

Once a return is approved and the item has been received and inspected, refunds are typically processed within **5 to 7 business days**. Refunds are credited to the original payment method used at the time of purchase. Please allow an additional 2 to 5 business days for the credit to appear on your bank or card statement depending on your financial institution.

If your original payment method is no longer active, please contact our support team and we will arrange an alternative refund method such as store credit or a check payment.

## Wrong Item or Damaged on Arrival

If you received the wrong item or your item arrived damaged, please contact us immediately with photo evidence. In these cases, we will:

1. Arrange a free return pickup within 24 to 48 hours.
2. Ship the correct replacement item within 1 to 2 business days, or issue a full refund if the item is out of stock.
3. Open a support ticket to track the resolution and ensure follow-up.

These cases are treated as priority issues and are escalated automatically to our fulfillment team.

## Warranty Policy

All electronics and appliances purchased from AgentSupport come with a **1-year limited manufacturer's warranty** from the date of delivery. The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use conditions. It does not cover accidental damage, water damage, unauthorized repairs, or damage resulting from misuse.

To file a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order ID, a description of the defect, and photos or video of the issue. Our team will assess the claim and respond within 2 business days. Approved warranty claims may result in a repair, replacement, or refund at our discretion based on the nature and severity of the defect.

Extended warranty options are available for select product categories at the time of checkout.

## Non-Returnable Items

The following items are **not eligible for return or refund** unless they arrive defective or damaged:

- Digital downloads and software licenses
- Personalized or custom-made products
- Consumable items such as printer ink, batteries, and cleaning supplies (if opened)
- Gift cards and store credit
- Items explicitly marked as "Final Sale" or "Non-Returnable" at the time of purchase
- Perishable goods

## Exchanges

Exchanges are available for the same item in a different size or color, subject to availability. To request an exchange, contact our support team within 30 days of delivery. If the desired replacement is unavailable, we will issue a full refund.

## Order Cancellations

Orders may be cancelled free of charge within **2 hours of placement** as long as the item has not yet been shipped. To cancel an order, contact our support team immediately with your order ID. If the item has already shipped, you will need to initiate a return once it is delivered.

## Escalation Process

If your issue is not resolved within the standard timelines, or if you are dissatisfied with the initial resolution offered, you may request escalation to a senior support specialist. Escalated cases are reviewed within 24 hours and a dedicated specialist will contact you directly by email or phone.

To escalate a case, simply inform the support agent during your chat or call and reference your ticket ID. You can also email escalations@agentsupport.io directly with your ticket ID in the subject line.

We take all escalations seriously and aim to resolve them within 48 hours of escalation.

## Contact Us

Our support team is available Monday through Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM EST. You may reach us via the chat interface, email at support@agentsupport.io, or by phone at 1-800-555-0199. For urgent matters, priority support is available 24/7 through the chat interface for orders flagged as damaged, missing, or wrong item received.
